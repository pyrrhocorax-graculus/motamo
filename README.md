# Motamo - Vocabulaire

Motamo is a vocabulary learning app for iPhone, built around widgets: a word of the hour in the language
you learn, with its translation, on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. 4 600 words in 20 themes, levels A1
to C1, each with an example sentence, pronunciation and, for verbs, its conjugation. The app works
offline and needs no account. Interface in French; languages: French, English, German, Spanish,
Portuguese, Dutch and Swiss German.
